Continuing the auction
  • Overcaller has a maximum — bid on past a simple preference (raise responder's major, or bid game directly with a big two-suiter).
  • Overcaller is minimum — pass out responder's choice.
  • Opponents compete — a further double by either partner is for penalty; bids retain their natural meaning.
  • Balancing seat — the same structure applies, though many partnerships allow slightly lighter hands to protect.
