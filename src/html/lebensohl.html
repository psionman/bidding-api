Continuing the auction — both auctions
  • Partner relays with 2NT — bid 3♣ automatically; don't pass or bid anything else.
  • Partner passes your 3♣ — pass; they've shown a weak hand with no game try.
  • Partner bids a new suit over 3♣ — pass. It's a weak sign-off, not forward-going.
  • Partner bids 3NT over 3♣ — pass unless you have real extras beyond a bare minimum.
  • Partner cuebids the opponent's suit over 3♣ — forcing; show a stopper, extra shape, or values.
  • Partner bids a new suit directly (no 2NT first) — forcing; raise with support or bid on toward game/slam.
  • Partner bids 3NT directly (no 2NT first) — pass; they hold a stopper and want to play there.
Some partnerships let the doubler bypass the automatic 3♣ with a very strong hand and a great suit of their own — a partnership-specific exception. The standard treatment is that the 3♣ relay is always completed.
